THE GAME

The duck has a job.

It is not a very complicated job.

Find the stash

24 plants scattered across three dangerous worlds. Eight per world. A golden arrow points you toward the nearest one when you get lost.

Become fog

Collect the right power-up and become a translucent cloud. Phase through walls. Ignore water. Temporarily ignore reality.

Play as a duck

A hop, a sprint, and a dash that launches you at 900 units per second. No sword. No gun. Just duck.

UNDER THE HOOD

Surprisingly serious code.

The game looks chill. The code underneath is considerably less chill.

PuRlO is built from scratch in C++17 with SFML 2.6. No game engine. Every major system is custom-built.

Procedural tilemap generation, steering-based enemy AI with orbital flanking, smooth camera follow, dual-sine screen shake, and a full dialogue engine with typewriter text are all written specifically for the game.

Worlds are generated using seeded RNG, cluster-based terrain placement, random-walk path carving, and flood-fill reachability checks to make sure every collectible can actually be reached.

Collision uses iterative least-penetration resolution with per-axis sliding. Enemies separate to avoid stacking, recover after knockback, and perform line-of-sight checks before melee attacks.

FAQ

Ask the duck.

What are the controls?

WASD to move. Space to hop. Shift to sprint. K or Left Ctrl to dash. E to talk/interact. I to open inventory. Escape to close menu. R to restart after death.

How does the dash work?

Dash sends you at 900 units/sec for 0.2 seconds with a 0.6 second cooldown. You are invulnerable while dashing and deal damage to enemies on contact.

How many worlds are there?

Three: Grasslands, Desert, and Cave. Each world is 2000x2000 pixels and procedurally generated using seeded RNG. Eight plants to collect in each.

What power-ups can I find?

Speed Potion gives 1.35x move speed for six seconds. Ghost Shield gives full invulnerability for four seconds. Hearts increase your maximum HP. Weed triggers Fog Form.

What is Fog Form?

You become a translucent mist. Move at 420 u/s, pass through walls and water, and cannot take damage. You cannot dash or hop while in fog.

Does inventory pause the game?

No. The inventory is an overlay. Enemies keep moving and timers keep ticking while you decide what to use.

Is PuRlO free?

Yes. Free and open source. Download the Windows build or compile the project yourself using CMake and SFML 2.6.

Was this made with a game engine?

No. PuRlO is built from scratch in C++17 using SFML 2.6. Procedural generation, enemy AI, camera systems, dialogue, collision, and other core systems are all custom.
