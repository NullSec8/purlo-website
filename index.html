PuRlO

A chill quest for 8 plants — times 3

You're a brave little duck. A stoner NPC lost his stash of 24 plants across three dangerous worlds. Dash through enemies, phase through walls in fog form, and collect them all. No weapons — just a dash, a hop, and your reflexes.

What is PuRlO?

Explore

Three procedurally generated 2000x2000 worlds — grasslands, desert, and dark caves. Each biome has its own tileset, music track, and enemy roster. No two runs feel the same.

Survive

No weapons — just a dash, a hop, and your reflexes. Sprint through enemies, phase through walls in fog form, or time your dash for a 900-unit-per-second strike. Every enemy has unique AI.

Collect

8 plants hidden across each world. A golden arrow guides you to the nearest one. Bring them all back to the Stoner and he'll comp you with a fog form to reach the next level.

Enemies

Four enemy types with distinct AI. They flank, aggro nearby allies, and never stop hunting.

Skeleton

Melee pursuer. Chases at 135 u/s within 380px, flanks from the side, retreats after hitting. 1 dash to kill.

Bat

Flying. Ignores walls, water, and terrain entirely. Moves at 182 u/s — the fastest enemy. Cave biome only.

Slime

Stationary ranged attacker. Spits slime balls at 260 u/s from 480px away. 8 HP — the tankiest enemy in the game.

Brute

3 HP. Slightly slower but always drops a Heart on death. Starts appearing in World 2 and never lets up.

Under the Hood

PuRlO is built from scratch in C++17 with SFML 2.6 — no game engine. Every system is hand-written: procedural tilemap generation, steering-based enemy AI with orbital flanking, a camera system with smooth follow and dual-sine screen shake, and a full dialogue engine with typewriter text.

The worlds are generated using seeded RNG with cluster-based terrain placement, random-walk path carving, and flood-fill reachability checks to guarantee every collectible is accessible. Enemy placement uses a separate RNG pass to avoid locking to the tilemap seed.

Collision uses iterative least-penetration resolution with per-axis sliding. Enemies separate to avoid stacking, unwedge from terrain after knockback, and maintain line-of-sight checks before melee attacks.

Settings persist across sessions. The game letterboxes to 16:9 at any resolution from 720p to 1440p, with fullscreen and windowed modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the controls?

WASD — Move. Space — Hop (crosses water). Shift — Sprint. K or Left Ctrl — Dash (your attack). E — Talk/Interact. I — Inventory. Escape — Close menu. R — Restart on death.

How does the dash work?

Dash sends you at 900 units/sec for 0.2 seconds with a 0.6s cooldown. While dashing you're invulnerable and deal damage to enemies on contact. Each enemy can only be hit once per dash — hitting one triggers knockback and aggros nearby enemies within 260px.

How many levels are there?

3 worlds: Grassy (6 skeletons), Desert (8 skeletons + 3 brutes), and Cave (10 skeletons, 4 brutes, 5 bats, 2 slimes). Each is 2000x2000 pixels, procedurally generated with seeded RNG. 8 plants per world.

What do power-ups do?

Power-ups drop from enemies and go into your inventory (press I). Speed Potion: 1.35x move speed for 6 seconds. Ghost Shield: full invulnerability for 4 seconds. Heart: +1 max HP (capped at 10). Weed: Fog Form — phase through all terrain at 420 u/s for 8 seconds.

What is Fog Form?

A translucent mist state. You move at 420 u/s, pass through all walls and water, and can't take damage. You can't dash or hop while in fog. The Stoner gives it to you when you clear a level, and the rare Weed drop (12% chance) triggers it independently.

Does the game pause when I open inventory?

No. The inventory is an overlay — enemies keep moving and timers keep ticking while you browse your power-ups. Manage your items carefully mid-combat.

Is it free?

Yes. PuRlO is free and open source. Download the Windows build or compile from source with CMake and SFML 2.6.

How was this made?

C++17 with SFML 2.6 for rendering, input, and audio. CMake for builds. No game engine — procedural generation, enemy AI, camera systems, dialogue engine, and collision resolution are all custom.
