UNDER THE HOOD

Surprisingly serious code.

The game looks chill. The code underneath is considerably less chill.

PuRlO is built from scratch in C++17 with SFML 2.6. No game engine. Every major system is custom-built.

Procedural tilemap generation, steering-based enemy AI with orbital flanking, smooth camera follow, dual-sine screen shake, and a full dialogue engine with typewriter text are all written specifically for the game.

Worlds are generated using seeded RNG, cluster-based terrain placement, random-walk path carving, and flood-fill reachability checks to make sure every collectible can actually be reached.

Collision uses iterative least-penetration resolution with per-axis sliding. Enemies separate to avoid stacking, recover after knockback, and perform line-of-sight checks before melee attacks.

QUESTIONS?

Ask the duck.

What are the controls?

WASD — Move. Space — Hop. Shift — Sprint. K or Left Ctrl — Dash. E — Talk/Interact. I — Inventory. Escape — Close menu. R — Restart after death.

How does the dash work?

Dash sends you at 900 units/sec for 0.2 seconds with a 0.6 second cooldown. You're invulnerable while dashing and deal damage to enemies on contact.

How many worlds are there?

Three: Grasslands, Desert, and Cave. Each world is 2000×2000 pixels and procedurally generated using seeded RNG. There are eight plants to collect in each.

What power-ups can I find?

Speed Potion gives you 1.35× move speed for six seconds. Ghost Shield gives full invulnerability for four seconds. Hearts increase your maximum HP. Weed triggers Fog Form.

What is Fog Form?

You become a translucent mist. You move at 420 u/s, pass through walls and water, and cannot take damage. You can't dash or hop while in fog.

Does inventory pause the game?

Nope. The inventory is an overlay. Enemies keep moving and timers keep ticking while you decide what to use.

Is PuRlO actually free?

Yes. PuRlO is free and open source. Download the Windows build or compile the project yourself using CMake and SFML 2.6.

Was this made with a game engine?

Nope. PuRlO is built from scratch in C++17 using SFML 2.6. Procedural generation, enemy AI, camera systems, dialogue, collision, and other core systems are custom.
